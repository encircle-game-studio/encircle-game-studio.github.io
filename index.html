Encircle Game Studio

Encircle Game Studio is the games team of Encircle Technologies Private Limited in Rajkot, India. We make mobile games for iPhone, iPad and Android: a tower stacking game, a motorcycle fighting racer and an English word game for kids.

Games

Tower Tap

Rebuild famous towers from their blueprints, one floor at a time: a crane swings each floor across the sky and you tap to drop it. From the Great Pyramid and Qutub Minar to modern skyscrapers.
App Store · Google Play · Privacy policy

Highway Brawlers

A 3D motorcycle combat racing game: fight rival bikers with fists and kicks at full speed, and the riders you hit remember you. Ride the Long Run across 25 highways in 5 regions, and races play offline.
App Store · Google Play · Privacy policy

Letter Leaves

An English spelling, phonics and reading game for kids aged 5 to 11, where every word your child learns grows their own garden. It plays offline, needs no account and collects nothing.
